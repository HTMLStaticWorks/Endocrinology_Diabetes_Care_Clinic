Clinical Philosophy

Beyond Symptom Management: Root-Cause Biochemistry

Diagnostic Excellence

In-House Laboratory & Ultrasound Technology

Delivering precision care requires immediate diagnostic feedback. Our facility houses high-complexity automated immunoassay instruments and neck sonography suites, eliminating the 2-week waiting periods common in external referral systems.

  • Roche Cobas & Beckman Coulter Analyzers: High-sensitivity chemiluminescent immunoassays for Free T3, Free T4, TSH, Cortisol, and C-Peptide.
  • GE Logiq E10 Ultrasound: Micro-vascular color Doppler and shear-wave elastography for thyroid nodule stratification.
  • Direct Electronic Medical Record Sync: Automated telemetry data integration into your encrypted health record.
