Signature Clinical Pathway

Metabolic Health Timeline

An integrated, step-by-step clinical progression designed for sustained hormonal balance and glycemic stability.

Stage 01 · Initial Clinical Assessment

Deep-Dive Metabolic Consultation

We review your complete endocrine history, family predisposition, metabolic symptoms, medication adherence, and lifestyle biometrics with an experienced endocrinologist.

  • 45-minute comprehensive specialist intake
  • Detailed symptom mapping (energy crashes, weight changes, thermoregulation)
  • Digital onboarding to our secure patient portal
Book Stage 1 Intake
Stage 02 · CLIA Laboratory Diagnostics

Precision Endocrine & Biomarker Panel

On-site CLIA-certified phlebotomy and rapid endocrine assay testing. We evaluate full thyroid panels, HbA1c, fasting insulin, cortisol rhythm, and adrenal markers.

  • High-sensitivity TSH, Free T3, Free T4, and Thyroid Antibodies (TPO/TgAb)
  • Fasting C-peptide, lipid subfractions, and microalbuminuria screening
  • Same-day or 24-hour turnaround directly to your portal
Explore Lab Capabilities
Stage 03 · Specialized Evaluation

Phenotype & Etiology Mapping

Going beyond generic labels. We pinpoint the exact root cause—whether Hashimoto's autoimmune thyroiditis, insulin resistance, LADA, or pituitary dysfunction.

  • High-resolution thyroid ultrasound and nodule classification (TI-RADS)
  • Insulin sensitivity indices (HOMA-IR) and glycemic variability indexing
  • Clear, empathetic explanation of clinical findings
Meet Our Diagnosticians
Stage 04 · Targeted Therapeutics

Personalized Protocol Formulation

We combine modern pharmacotherapy (GLP-1 RAs, SGLT2i, optimized levothyroxine/liothyronine, bioidentical HRT) with precision medical nutrition.

  • Custom titration schedules to minimize side-effects
  • CGM (Continuous Glucose Monitor) sensor placement & calibration
  • Dedicated diabetes educator & registered dietitian coordination
Review Treatment Plans
Stage 05 · Continuous Telemetry

Real-Time Data-Driven Tracking

Our clinical team reviews your CGM telemetry, blood glucose logs, and symptom diaries asynchronously through our integrated patient portal.

  • Real-time alerts for unexpected hypoglycemic or hyperglycemic excursions
  • Automated medication schedule reminders & refill tracking
  • In-app secure doctor messaging for quick protocol adjustments
Open Patient Telemetry Demo
Stage 06 · Long-Term Metabolic Wellness

Quarterly Optimization & Remission Care

Scheduled quarterly review visits to adjust dosages, repeat ultrasound imaging, celebrate HbA1c reductions, and maintain lifelong metabolic vitality.

  • Longitudinal lab result trend comparisons across months & years
  • Diabetes reversal & medication de-escalation protocols
  • Preventive cardiovascular & bone density surveillance
Schedule Annual Review
