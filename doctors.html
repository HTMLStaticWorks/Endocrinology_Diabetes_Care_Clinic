Patient Guidance

Seeking a Specialist Second Opinion?

If you have received an ambiguous thyroid ultrasound, struggling with uncontrolled glucose despite medications, or experiencing unexplained fatigue, our board-certified endocrinologists provide thorough case reviews.

  • Complete diagnostic record & imaging audit
  • Independent molecular testing and risk classification
  • Telehealth second opinion visits available nationwide
Request Second Opinion Intake

Intake Checklist for New Patients

1. Previous Lab Records (Past 12 Months)

TSH, Free T3/T4, HbA1c, fasting insulin, lipid panel, and antibody tests.

2. Imaging & Biopsy Reports

Ultrasound reports, thyroid scan discs, or fine needle biopsy pathology.

3. Current Medication & Dose Schedule

Exact dosages and brands of all prescription hormones, supplements, and vitamins.
