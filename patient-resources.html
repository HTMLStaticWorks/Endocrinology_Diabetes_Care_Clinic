Self-Assessment Tool

Endocrine Symptom Explorer

Identify whether your symptoms suggest thyroid, glycemic, or adrenal imbalance.

Thyroid Axis
Hypo / Hyperthyroidism
  • Cold intolerance or heat sensitivity
  • Unexplained hair thinning or outer eyebrow loss
  • Fatigue, sluggish metabolism, constipation
  • Palpitations or anterior neck fullness
Thyroid Diagnostic Panel
Glycemic Axis
Insulin Resistance & Diabetes
  • Postprandial brain fog and energy crashes
  • Increased thirst and frequent urination
  • Persistent central abdominal weight gain
  • Dark velvety skin patches (Acanthosis)
Glycemic & CGM Evaluation
Adrenal & Hormone
Cortisol & Sex Hormones
  • Chronic sleep disturbance & high evening alertness
  • Low libido, hot flashes, or cycle irregularity
  • Sarcopenia or reduced exercise recovery
  • Mood volatility and salt cravings
