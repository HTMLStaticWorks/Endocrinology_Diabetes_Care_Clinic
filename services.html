Clinical Programs

Specialist Clinical Services

State-of-the-art diagnostic evaluations and personalized therapeutic protocols for complex endocrine, thyroid, hormonal, and metabolic conditions.

Pillar 01

Advanced Diabetes & Glycemic Telemetry

Managing diabetes requires more than isolated A1c checks. We integrate modern Continuous Glucose Monitoring (Dexcom G7, FreeStyle Libre), hybrid closed-loop insulin pumps (Omnipod, Tandem Control-IQ), and evidence-based pharmacotherapies.

  • Type 1 Diabetes: Advanced automated insulin delivery and hypoglycemia mitigation.
  • Type 2 Diabetes: Insulin resistance reversal protocols, GLP-1 RAs, and SGLT2 inhibitors.
  • LADA & Maturity Onset: Precise autoantibody biomarker classification.
  • Gestational Diabetes: High-risk maternal metabolic surveillance.
Book Diabetes Consultation
Pillar 02

Comprehensive Thyroid & Nodule Care

From autoimmune Hashimoto’s thyroiditis to nodular goiters, our clinic provides in-office high-resolution thyroid ultrasound and ultrasound-guided Fine Needle Aspiration (FNA) biopsy.

  • Autoimmune Thyroiditis: TPO and Thyroglobulin antibody tracking with optimized T4/T3 combination therapy.
  • Graves’ Hyperthyroidism: Anti-thyroid medication titration and radioiodine coordination.
  • Thyroid Ultrasound & Biopsy: On-site TI-RADS risk classification and molecular testing.
Schedule Thyroid Assessment
Pillar 03

Bioidentical Hormone Balancing & Pituitary Care

Hormonal imbalances cascade throughout every physiological system. We formulate tailored regimens for men and women experiencing endocrine deficiency or dysregulation.

  • PCOS (Polycystic Ovary Syndrome): Restoring ovulatory cycles and metabolic balance.
  • Menopause & Andropause: Evidence-supported hormone replacement therapy (HRT/TRT).
  • Adrenal & Pituitary Disorders: Cushing's syndrome, Addison's disease, and prolactinomas.
Request Hormone Evaluation
Pillar 04

Physician-Led Metabolic & Weight Optimization

Treating obesity as a chronic, neuro-endocrine disease. We provide medical nutrition therapy combined with modern GLP-1/GIP receptor agonists (Semaglutide, Tirzepatide) under close physician supervision.

  • Metabolic Syndrome: Reversing insulin resistance, elevated triglycerides, and visceral adiposity.
  • Cardio-Metabolic Risk: Advanced lipid fractionation (ApoB, Lp(a)) and endothelial health.
  • Fatty Liver (MASLD/MASH): Proactive hepatic fibrosis prevention.
Join Metabolic Program
Pillar 05

Bone Mineral Metabolism & Osteoporosis

Comprehensive skeletal health evaluations for post-menopausal women, men with low bone density, and patients with parathyroid disorders or chronic steroid use.

  • DEXA Interpretation: T-score and Z-score fracture risk modeling (FRAX).
  • Anabolic & Antiresorptive Therapies: Prolia, Evenity, Tymlos, and Reclast infusions.
  • Hyperparathyroidism & Calcium: In-depth calcium and vitamin D metabolism evaluation.
Book Bone Health Review
