Endocrine Mapping

Metabolic Phenotyping & Biomarkers

Targeting the four key biological axes that regulate your energy and metabolic vitality.

Axis 01
Insulin Sensitivity

HOMA-IR calculation, fasting C-peptide ratios, and postprandial glycemic excursions to reverse metabolic resistance.

Target HOMA-IR < 1.5
Axis 02
Thyroid Antibodies

Quantitative tracking of TPO, Thyroglobulin Antibodies, and Free T3/T4 conversion efficiency for Hashimoto's care.

Target TSH 1.0 – 2.0 mIU/L
Axis 03
ApoB & Lipid Subfractions

Moving past simple LDL-C to measure atherogenic particle concentration (ApoB, Lp(a)) and triglyceride/HDL balance.

Target ApoB < 70 mg/dL
Axis 04
Adrenal Cortisol Rhythm

4-point salivary/serum cortisol rhythm analysis to evaluate chronic neuro-endocrine stress and diurnal disruption.
