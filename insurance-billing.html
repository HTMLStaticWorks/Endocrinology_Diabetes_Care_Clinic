Consumer Protection

Your Rights: Upfront & Itemized Billing

Under the federal No Surprises Act, health care providers must give patients who do not have insurance or who are not using insurance an estimate of the bill for medical items and services.

  • Good Faith Estimate (GFE): Provided in writing at least 1 business day before your scheduled clinical visit.
  • No Hidden Facility Surcharges: You will only be billed for physician professional fees and specific on-site diagnostic assays.
  • Dispute Resolution Rights: If your final bill is $400 or more above your GFE, you have federal rights to dispute.
